Harbor — a reply, end to end

A support queue is one place, and Harbor keeps the state of every conversation in it.

Mode  walkthrough Audience  Support leads evaluating a helpdesk; watching muted on a phone. Runtime  23.3s Formats  phone, wide Sound  silent, with cuts on the click and the send

The app on screen is Harbor, a fictional support desk. Every state is reached by a real click on the real element; the loading state and the toast are the app's own.
